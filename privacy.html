隐私政策

适用于 iOS / iPadOS App「爆炸图」。生效日期:2026 年 10 月 1 日。

一句话

爆炸图不收集、不上传、不分享任何个人信息。没有账号、没有广告、没有第三方统计或分析工具。

保存在您设备上的内容

为了让 App 正常工作,以下三项只保存在您的设备上(iOS 的 UserDefaults),不会离开设备,我们也看不到:

  • 界面与讲解的语言(中文或英文);
  • 孩子完成"找一找"后得到的星标;
  • 上一次查询到的解锁状态(用于打开 App 时立即显示书架)。

删除 App 即可清除以上全部内容。

购买与订阅

订阅与购买全部由 Apple 的 App Store 处理。App 只向 Apple 查询"是否已解锁",我们不会收到您的姓名、Apple 账户、付款信息或任何可识别身份的信息。管理或取消订阅请前往设备的"设置 > Apple 账户 > 订阅"。

网络

除 App Store 购买与解锁查询外,App 不发出任何网络请求。所有物件模型、讲解与配音都随 App 安装在设备上。

儿童隐私

本 App 面向儿童设计。我们不向儿童收集任何个人信息。购买、订阅管理与一切外部链接都位于"家长门"之后,需要家长答题才能进入。

政策变更

若本政策有变,我们会更新本页并修改生效日期;如涉及收集数据方式的变化,会先在 App 更新说明中告知。

联系我们

Privacy Policy

For the iOS / iPadOS app "Take It Apart" (爆炸图). Effective October 1, 2026.

In short

Take It Apart does not collect, upload, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, and no third-party analytics.

What stays on your device

To work properly, the app keeps three things on your device only (in iOS UserDefaults). They never leave the device and we cannot see them:

  • the language for the interface and narration (Chinese or English);
  • the stars your child earns in Find It;
  • the last known unlock status, so the shelf can appear right away.

Deleting the app removes all of it.

Purchases and subscriptions

All purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple's App Store. The app only asks Apple whether content is unlocked. We never receive your name, Apple Account, payment details, or anything that identifies you. To manage or cancel a subscription, go to Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions on your device.

Network

Apart from App Store purchases and unlock checks, the app makes no network requests. All models, narration, and audio are installed with the app.

Children's privacy

This app is designed for children. We do not collect any personal information from children. Purchases, subscription management, and all external links sit behind a parental gate that only a grown-up can answer.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Any change to how data is handled will be announced in the app's release notes first.
